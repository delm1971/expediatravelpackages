The Last-Minute Realities: Price Creep vs. Unsold Capacity

Finding real price drops right before departure requires an analytical approach. You have to understand how airline revenue management systems handle final seating charts as the plane gets closer to taking off.

Carriers use historical data models to guess booking patterns. If a flight has plenty of empty seats within days of departure, automated systems often override standard premium markdowns. This creates a brief window where unallocated seats are priced low to fill the aircraft before it pushes back from the gate.
