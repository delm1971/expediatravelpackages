The Mechanics of Bundle Discounts: How Money Is Saved

To consistently track down genuine discounts, it helps to realize how online booking engines work behind the scenes. Hotel properties and commercial air carriers regularly deal with unsold capacity. To fill empty rooms and airplane cabins without ruining their standard brand value by running cheap public sales, they dump excess inventory into wholesale pools.

When you utilize an integrated booking matrix, these savings are blended seamlessly into a total package cost. Because the independent price of the flight or hotel is completely hidden inside the dynamic bundle price, suppliers safely give you steep markdowns while leaving their retail pricing looking completely untouched to the casual shopper.
